## Short description (max 80)

Control your Mitsubishi WF-RAC air conditioner on your home Wi-Fi. No cloud.

## Full description (max 4000)

AirLAN is a simple remote for Mitsubishi Heavy Industries air conditioners with the WF-RAC Wi-Fi module (the module used by the Smart M-Air app). It talks to your air conditioners directly over your home network: no account, no cloud, no waiting for a server.

What you can do, per air conditioner:
• Switch it on or off
• Choose cooling or heating
• Set the temperature in half degrees
• Have it switch off automatically after 30 minutes or 1 to 6 hours

That is all, on purpose. AirLAN finds the air conditioners on your network by itself and asks you to name them. You can also add one by IP address, for example when your smart devices live on a separate network.

The switch-off timer runs on your phone as an alarm, so it also works with the app closed and after a restart. If the air conditioner cannot be reached when the timer goes off, AirLAN keeps trying for ten minutes and tells you if it did not work.

Good to know:
• AirLAN works on your home network only. Away from home it cannot reach your air conditioners.
• Your air conditioners must already be connected to Wi-Fi, for example with the Smart M-Air app.
• Tested with the WF-RAC firmware that uses plain HTTP. Newer HTTPS firmware is supported but has not been tested on real units yet; reports are welcome on GitHub.
