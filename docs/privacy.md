# AirLAN privacy policy

*Last updated: 5 October 2026*

AirLAN controls Mitsubishi Heavy Industries air conditioners with the WF-RAC Wi-Fi module
directly over your home network. It is made by Anne Erdtsieck-Wijnen as an open-source
project: <https://github.com/erdtsieck/airlan>.

## What AirLAN collects

Nothing. AirLAN has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and
it does not send any data to the developer or to any other party.

## What AirLAN stores on your phone

- The air conditioners you added: their identifier, network address and the names you gave
  them.
- When a switch-off timer is set, the time at which it fires.
- A random identifier that AirLAN registers with each air conditioner. The air conditioner
  needs it to accept commands; it is not linked to you.

This stays on your phone. If you use Android's backup, the list of air conditioners and
their names is included in it, so a new phone knows them; that backup is handled by
Google under your Google account, not by AirLAN.

## Network access

AirLAN only talks to devices on your local network: it looks for air conditioners there
and sends them commands. It does not connect to the internet.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Nearby devices (local network) | To find your air conditioners and control them over your home network. |
| Alarms & reminders | To switch an air conditioner off at the exact time you chose. |
| Notifications | Only to tell you when a timer could not switch an air conditioner off. |
| Run at startup | To set your timers again after the phone restarts. |

## Children

AirLAN is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the project's history on GitHub.
